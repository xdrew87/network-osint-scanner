# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We pledge that everyone participating in our project or its community is respected and safe.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Supporting ethical use of technology

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination
- Insulting or derogatory comments
- Offensive or discriminatory jokes
- Unwelcome sexual advances or attention
- Publishing others' private information
- Encouraging illegal activities
- Any form of violence
- Using the tool for unauthorized access or malicious purposes

## Enforcement

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.

### Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, or have concerns:

1. Document the incident (dates, participants, description)
2. Contact the maintainers privately
3. Provide context and details
4. Do not escalate publicly unless necessary

### Consequences

Maintainers will address reported violations. Potential actions include:

1. **Warning** - First time minor violations
2. **Temporary Ban** - Repeated violations or serious incidents
3. **Permanent Ban** - Severe or repeated harassment

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project spaces (GitHub, documentation, discussions)
- Official project events and meetings
- Community spaces representing the project
- When acting as a representative

## Legal & Ethical Guidelines

### Responsible Use Commitment

All participants agree to:
- Use this tool only for authorized testing
- Respect privacy and legal boundaries
- Obtain written permission before scanning
- Comply with applicable laws
- Report vulnerabilities responsibly
- Never use for malicious purposes

### No Tolerance for Misuse

The project maintainers will **not tolerate** or support:
- Unauthorized network access or intrusions
- Data theft or privacy violations
- Disruption of services
- System damage or defacement
- Use against vulnerable individuals or organizations
- Any illegal activity

## Community Values

### Respect
- Treat all members with dignity
- Value diverse perspectives
- Welcome newcomers and help them learn

### Collaboration
- Work together constructively
- Share knowledge and experience
- Support each other's growth

### Integrity
- Be honest and transparent
- Follow through on commitments
- Acknowledge mistakes and learn

### Safety
- Create a safe space for everyone
- Report concerns promptly
- Respect boundaries

### Legality
- Follow all applicable laws
- Respect intellectual property
- Conduct authorized testing only

---

## Consequences for Code of Conduct Violations

### Level 1: Minor Violations
- Off-topic discussions
- Minor disrespect
- **Action**: Polite reminder via private message

### Level 2: Moderate Violations
- Persistent off-topic behavior
- Disruptive comments
- **Action**: Public warning, encouraged to edit/delete

### Level 3: Serious Violations
- Harassment or discrimination
- Abuse or insults
- **Action**: Temporary ban (24 hours - 7 days)

### Level 4: Severe Violations
- Severe harassment or threats
- Encouraging illegal activity
- Hate speech or discrimination
- **Action**: Permanent ban
